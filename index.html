Teacher instructions

Best phone workflow: AirDrop or message this HTML file to students. On iPhone, they can open it in Safari, tap Share, then choose “Add to Home Screen” for an app-like button.

Variety: press “New Random Card” before sharing to create a different layout. Students can also generate their own, but teachers may prefer to send a prepared card to each group.

Marking: students tap squares. Their phone remembers marks locally, even if they close and reopen the file.

Proof option: students can take a screenshot when they get bingo and send it to the teacher.

Edit the item bank: open this file in Notepad, TextEdit, VS Code, or any plain-text editor. Find the section named ITEM_BANK, then add, remove, or rewrite items. Keep quotation marks and commas intact.
